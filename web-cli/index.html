Engineered to Crush Bufferbloat & Keep NAT Alive

Developed under real-world cellular broadband conditions in Malabe, Sri Lanka on the Hutch 4G 20Mbps Unlimited plan. Here is how three targeted optimizations deliver up to 340% lower loaded latency.

BBR Congestion Control

Replaces legacy Reno/Cubic loss-based algorithms with model-based bottleneck bandwidth and round-trip propagation time calculations. Eliminates queue buildup in cellular eNodeB buffers.

net.ipv4.tcp_congestion_control = bbr
net.core.default_qdisc = fq

1420 MTU GTP Clamping

LTE Carrier EPC backhauls wrap packets in GPRS Tunneling Protocol (GTP-U) headers. Standard 1500 MTU packets fragment at the tower. Clamping to 1420 prevents fragmentation entirely.

ip link set dev wlan0 mtu 1420
iptables -t mangle -A POSTROUTING -p tcp -j TCPMSS --clamp-mss-to-pmtu

Carrier NAT Keepalive Pulse

Mobile providers tear down idle UDP connection states after 30-60 seconds, forcing radio reconnection stalls. Our 28-byte pulse fires every 25s, keeping the radio active for zero lag.

tplink-cli pulse --interval 25s
